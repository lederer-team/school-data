# The Lederer Team: Bay Area school data

Data files behind the school finder on [theledererteam.com](https://theledererteam.com).

- `eastbay.json`: public, charter and private schools in Alameda and Contra Costa counties
- `districts-eastbay.json`: school district boundaries (GeoJSON)

## Sources (public data)
- California Department of Education: School Directory, Census Day Enrollment 2025-26, CAASPP 2024-25 results, College-Going Rate (class of 2022-23), Private School Affidavit 2025-26, District Areas 2025-26
- U.S. Department of Education, NCES Private School Survey 2023-24

The 1-10 ratings are The Lederer Team's calculation: each school's average share of students meeting the state standard in English and math, ranked against California public schools at the same level.
